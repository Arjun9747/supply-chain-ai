Section 1: General Shipping Guidelines
All international shipments originating from Asia-Pacific must pass through customs clearance at the primary port of entry within 48 hours of arrival. Express freight packages are prioritized for automated sorting.

Section 2: Delay Protocols & Exceptions
In the event of severe weather or port congestion, logistics coordinators must notify regional managers immediately. Alternative routing through secondary hubs in Europe or North America is authorized if delays exceed 72 hours.

Section 3: Temperature-Sensitive Cargo
Pharmaceuticals and perishable goods require continuous temperature monitoring during transit. Any deviation outside the 2 to 8 degrees Celsius range must trigger an automated safety alert to quality assurance.
